About BASSIANA

In order to understanding how ecosystems respond to change at local, regional, and global scales, it is crucial to manage sustainably the ecosystems, govern the services they provide, and assess the socio-environmental implications for future development. In this context, the BASSIANA Marine Ecosystems DB was created to better understand and follow the changes occurring in ecosystems.

The BASSIANA Ecosystems Database is created in 2020 as an effort to collect Long-Term data and centralize the information related to the marine ecosystems in general.

BASSIANA Ecosystems Database was funded by the United States Agency for Development (USAID, USA) in the frame of the IMAS-Ichkeul project (AID-OAA-A-11-00012), managed by National Academy of Sciences, under the Partnerships for Enhanced Engagement in Research (PEER), Cycle 8 program & coordinated by the National Institute of Marine Sciences and Technologies, INSTM, Tunisia.

The Ecosystems Database provides a structure gathering vast amounts of high-quality data with efficient access. It holds over 50 environmental parameters (Biological, Chemical, Physical, Physico-chemical, Sedimentological and Fishery data). The data concern freshwater and marine ecosystems such as streams, rivers, lakes, and lagoons.

Our Mission

Gather the data of different type of Marine and coastal ecosystems.

Creating a centralized data repository for further use.

Have a preliminary incite on the spatial and temporal variabilities of the ecosystem's status applying statistical and numerical tools.

Provide an updated and accessible data for policy makers, environmental managers, researchers, and marine ecosystem stakeholders.
