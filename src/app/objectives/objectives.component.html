Sustainable Development Vision

🌍

The main keys for sustainable development in Ichkeul region is management of water budget in Lake. Controlling the water level in Lake will help to safeguard biodiversity in this ecosystem well known by its high biodiversity and promote a sustainable development (fishery and tourism).

🐟 Fishery Development
🏖️ Tourism Promotion

Specific Objectives

The specific objective of IMAS-Ichkeul project is to manage the water budget in Lake based on numerical approach and knowledge based on measurement thought the development of a new toolkit encompassing:

01
🛰️

Remote Sensing & GIS Database

A remote sensing and GIS data base needed for both models.

Satellite Data Geographic Information Spatial Analysis
02
💧

SWAT Model Integration

The newest version of SWAT model to provide river inputs into Lake.

River Flow Watershed Modeling Hydrological Analysis
03
🌊

Hydrodynamic Modeling

A one-dimensional hydrodynamic model to simulate the exchange of water between Ichkeul Lake and Bizerte Lagoon.

Water Exchange Flow Simulation Lake Dynamics
