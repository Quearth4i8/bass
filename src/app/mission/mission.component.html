Mission & Goals

Broadly, main goals of BASSIANA Database are:

📊

Data Collection

Gather data of different type of Marine and coastal ecosystems.

🗄️

Centralized Repository

Creating a centralized data repository for further use.

🔍

Ecosystem Analysis

Have a preliminary incite on spatial and temporal variabilities of ecosystem's status applying statistical and numerical tools.

👥

Stakeholder Access

Provide an updated and accessible data for policy makers, environmental managers, researchers, and marine ecosystem stakeholders.
