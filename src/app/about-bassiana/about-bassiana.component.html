Ecosystem Understanding

In order to understanding how ecosystems respond to change at local, regional, and global scales, it is crucial to manage sustainably ecosystems, govern the services they provide, and assess the socio-environmental implications for future development. In this context, BASSIANA Marine Ecosystems DB was created to better understand and follow the changes occurring in ecosystems.

🗄️

Database Creation

The BASSIANA Ecosystems Database is created in 2020 as an effort to collect Long-Term data and centralize information related to marine ecosystems in general.

💰

Funding & Partnerships

BASSIANA Ecosystems Database was funded by United States Agency for Development (USAID, USA) in the frame of the IMAS-Ichkeul project (AID-OAA-A-11-00012), managed by the National Academy of Sciences, under the Partnerships for Enhanced Engagement in Research (PEER), Cycle 8 program & coordinated by the National Institute of Marine Sciences and Technologies, INSTM, Tunisia.

Comprehensive Data Collection

The Ecosystems Database provides a structure gathering vast amounts of high-quality data with efficient access. It holds over 50 environmental parameters (Biological, Chemical, Physical, Physico-chemical, Sedimentological and Fishery data). The data concern freshwater and marine ecosystems such as streams, rivers, lakes, and lagoons. These different data are provided from several national and international projects and initiatives.
